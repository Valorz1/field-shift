# Demo script

A 2-minute walk through Rotara for judges. Everything runs on the live site: https://rotara-chi.vercel.app (no account needed).

## 1. Find a field (15 s)

Open the live site and go to the **Dashboard**. Type the postcode **NR19** (Dereham, Norfolk, UK) and press **Find**, or tap the **Norfolk** example. A pin drops on the map.

## 2. Pick the crop grown now (5 s)

Tap **Wheat**.

## 3. See what changed (30 s)

Scroll to **What is happening on this field**. Rotara compares 2021–2025 with 2001–2020 using 25 years of NASA POWER data:

- Yearly rain fell only 2% (706 mm to 693 mm)...
- ...but summer rain fell 30% (206 mm to 145 mm), right when crops need it.

The rain chart also shows NASA GPM IMERG satellite rain as an independent check (Norfolk 706 vs 735 mm a year). The **Soil Intelligence** page adds NASA SMAP soil moisture.

## 4. Tell Rotara what matters (15 s)

Under **About your farm**, pick priorities such as **Use less water** and **Build organic matter**, plus water, machinery, buyer and soil type.

## 5. Compare 3 plans for the next 3 years (40 s)

Open **Strategies** (or **Compare** for a side-by-side table). The 3 plans are:

1. **Keep the current crop**
2. **Mix in climate-suited crops**
3. **Build the soil** with a cover crop

Each plan shows which priorities it meets, what to check first, the cited rule behind it (for example, AHDB's 5-year gap between pulse crops) and what it does not know. One plan is marked **Best fit** for the farmer's answers. The plans come from cited rules, not AI.

## 6. Try a dry farm (10 s)

Go back to the Dashboard, tap **Western Nevada** and pick **Alfalfa**. The plans change: Nevada gives alfalfa, then cowpea, then sorghum. The Soil page explains why SMAP is not used there (0 of 1,492 readings had recommended quality).

## 7. Trust and disclosure (5 s)

Open **About & disclosure** in the sidebar: data sources, privacy, credits and how AI was used.
